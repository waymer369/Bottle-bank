How it works

One currency, two ways to spend it

Every purchase deposits points into your account at a fixed rate — 10 points for every dollar spent. From there, it's yours to manage.

①

Buy with cash

Pay full price for a drink and points land in your account automatically — no enrollment, no card to carry.

②

Redeem for a drink

Have enough points saved up? Cash them in for a drink on the house, straight from your balance.

③

Move to savings

Not thirsty? Transfer points into your cash savings jar and withdraw the value for anything else, anytime.
